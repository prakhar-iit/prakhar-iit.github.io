Retrieval Stack Study

Benchmarked six retrieval methods — BM25, Word2Vec, sentence-transformers, Matryoshka embeddings, hybrid fusion and cross-encoder reranking — on 20K arXiv abstracts with a 400-query eval set.

  • 0.9885 nDCG@10 for hybrid fusion, best of all methods
